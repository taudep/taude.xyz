Hi, I'm Taude (pronounced like Maude, but with a T).

I formerly blogged at [Simpliefied Chaos](https://simplifiedchaos.tumblr.com/) during the peak blogging era of rich Internet apps, my [cycling](https://doublehop.blogspot.com/2009/09/more-bacons-2009-vermont-50-race-report.html) [shenanigans](https://doublehop.blogspot.com/2010/08/2010-d2r2-randonnee-ride-report.html) on [double-hop photos](https://doublehop.blogspot.com/2010/12/20skulls-end-of-season-thoughts.html), that eventually morphed into #20poundskull and [covering cyclocross for various media](https://www.google.com/search?q=todd+Prekaski+cyclocross+site%3Ahttps%3A%2F%2Fvelo.outsideonline.com&sca_esv=8371335fcf8fca6e&biw=1355&bih=730&sxsrf=APpeQns7SvXxMjc5PbhOd8TADIAeNZ08tw%3A1784731507796&ei=c9dgaoCPMJDk5NoPn4y08Q0&ved=0ahUKEwjAlKr8wuaVAxUQMlkFHR8GLd4Q4dUDCBA&uact=5&oq=todd+Prekaski+cyclocross+site%3Ahttps%3A%2F%2Fvelo.outsideonline.com&gs_lp=Egxnd3Mtd2l6LXNlcnAiPHRvZGQgUHJla2Fza2kgY3ljbG9jcm9zcyBzaXRlOmh0dHBzOi8vdmVsby5vdXRzaWRlb25saW5lLmNvbUjmaVCcAViRZXABeAGQAQCYAWegAYMOqgEEMzAuM7gBA8gBAPgBAvgBAZgCAaACBMICChAAGEcY1gQYsAPCAg0QABiABBiKBRhDGLADwgIZEC4YgAQYigUYQxjHARjRAxjIAxiwA9gBAcICGRAuGEMYxwEY0QMYgAQYigUYyAMYsAPYAQGYAwCIBgGQBgu6BgQIARgIkgcBMaAHuxSyBwC4BwDCBwMyLTHIBwOACAE&sclient=gws-wiz-serp)outlets, traveling to Europe to make the cult classic, and greatest cyclocross movie of all time, Das Pro und The Rookie (would link that, but Vimeo changes their hosting policies and it's currently locked down).

Other then my former cycling glory, I write about the things I'm learning, currently around AI (like everyone else), and software, management (can't believe I've been managing at various org-levels for over 11 years now) plus whatever else sticks: quick **TIL** notes, longer **posts**, and short **quotes** worth remembering. 

All my writing in "posts" will be my own, but there will be some ai-slop, here and there, and it'll be labeled as such, since part of the purpose of this blog is to publish all the things I need to remember.  A big inspiration for all this is  [Simon Willison's blog](https://simonwillison.net/about/).

I really want to get back to creating a part of the web that's mine, and not controlled by any social media algorithm.   The new era of AI and agents, and easy automation is enabling this.
