# Archivore 

[Archivore](https://github.com/taudep/archivore) is an attempt to automate information gathering from things I've read on the web.  It's goal is to search my browser history (usually that's kept in a Sqlite database), and to harvest all the links I've gone to at news.ycombinator, reddit (list of technology sub-reddits I follow), or interesting tweets, and automatically build and maintain my Karpathy inspired [LLM Wiki](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f).   
* Future Goals include: automatic writing, summarization of findings into ai-slop, posting on various social media sites, and generic bot-behavior, but hopefully very fine-tuned that it's not a total mess.
