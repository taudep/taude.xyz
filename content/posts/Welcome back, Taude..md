I have so many ideas, that I'm now able to experiment with.  I was always first a product builder and engineer second. With AI, I'm able to building tools, experiment, publish summaries and findings, and all that good mundane stuff that AI simplifies, while managing teams and all that people-stuff.

I really want to get back to creating a part of the web that's mine, not controlled by any social media algorithm.
